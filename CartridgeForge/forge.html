Synonym banks used by the procedural template mutator. The engine picks one entry per [bank_name] marker in a template. Keep entries short.

Override the generic dialogue pack with your character's own templates, keyword patterns, goals, and fallback lines. Leave sections untouched to use the generic pack — the cartridge will work either way. Placeholders {address}, {topic}, {memory}, {name} are filled by the engine at runtime; [bank_name] inserts a random entry from a synonym bank.

Templates

Lines the character says, indexed by group (greeting / praise / insult / …) and intent. Engine picks among matching templates by current plan + register.

Keyword patterns

Substrings of the user's input that tag it with a class (praise / insult / threat / question / …), assign a group, and shift VAD. Order matters — earlier rows win.

Goals

Each goal pulls toward an intent when its drive-weight vector aligns with the character's current drive deltas. Higher priority = stronger pull.

Fallback lines

Used when no template scores high enough. Tier 1 = neutral ("tell me more"), tier 2 = recovering ("hmm. where was I"), tier 3 = checked-out ("...").

Voice LM

Train a small character-level n-gram language model on text written in your character's voice. The engine uses this LM to rerank candidate replies — utterances that look like your training text score higher than ones that don't. This is what gives Pretorius his gravitas and Kiki her playfulness.

Paste 500–5000 characters of dialogue, monologue, or sample writing in this character's voice. More text = sharper voice. Skip if you don't care — the cartridge runs fine without an LM.

Test phrase

Type a phrase to see its per-character log-probability under your LM (milli-nats). Higher = more in-register. Built-in Pretorius scores ~-552; modern slang scores ~-3090.
