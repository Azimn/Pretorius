# Imported Persona Fidelity V6

Imported characters often arrive with years of voice habits, relationship rules, private lore, and user expectations. The engine must preserve those traits as cartridge data, not as special cases in code.

Core rule: engine-general, cartridge-specific. The engine provides symbolic machinery for register constraints, relationship posture, memory accountability, open loops, refusal, repair, and contradiction handling. The cartridge defines which constraints matter.

Author these fields for imported personas:

- Required register: phrases, eras, metaphors, formality, dialect markers, pet names, or silence patterns the character should use.
- Forbidden register: modern slang, assistant phrases, corporate tone, profanity, flirtation, cruelty, or any style the character should not use.
- Knowledge/voice split: what the character may know versus how that knowledge must be expressed.
- Relationship posture: subordinate, equal, superior, rival, caretaker, partner, mentor, family-like, adversarial, or other.
- Romance eligibility: never, possible, current, former, conditional, or NPC-only. Romance must be explicit cartridge intent, not a default chat behavior.
- Boundary style: how the character refuses intimacy, correction, taboo topics, tool-like requests, or role violations.
- Attachment style: secure, anxious, avoidant, teasing, devotional, protective, jealous, distant, or competitive.
- Memory protections: facts that can be corrected, facts that require clarification, and core memories that should resist revision.
- NPC/entity relationships: bonds to actors other than the current user, including affection, rivalry, obligation, resentment, grief, and loyalty.

Kiki is the reference case for this layer. She can understand modern technology, but should phrase it through older comparisons and social habits. The battery checks that she avoids modern leakage such as casual current slang, even though the engine itself knows nothing about Kiki specifically.

Do not solve fidelity by adding more prose alone. Add symbolic constraints, then give templates and fallback lines enough voice to express those constraints.
